## Impact on the system

### Evidence and findings

The driver receives one exact current screenshot. Analysis receives at most three relevant originals; review retrieves the exact analyst candidates and their original dependencies directly, capped at eight screens. Every role still reads the full original walk and navigator notes. The local shared pool retains all originals. A missing, changed or over-limit required source produces an incomplete check, never a replacement summary or guessed result.

New records bind their original source-link list inside their body fingerprint. Read records preserve the exact source edges. Publication traverses the entire cited graph, checks that the reviewer actually read its dependencies and refuses unstable or changed sources. A stable observation cannot hide an unstable underlying image. Unrelated unread damage does not withdraw an intact finding.

Element-specific model claims require the original screen and element identifiers. SAM uses that original even after navigation moves elsewhere. Accepted repeats group only when the declared problem, criterion, original page and browser target identity match; different problems or controls remain separate. Different measured/visual basis labels do not create extra cards or change the first accepted claim. Whole-view repeats group only with the same exact original sources, problem and criterion. Every raw claim and original occurrence stays saved. Whole-view judgement is supported without invented element identifiers.

Analysis and review instructions explicitly check surrounding visible context and the complete unchanged claim. A changed title cannot silently approve unsupported details. A new claim needs a supported-premise review. Older reviews without source-link records are withheld with an explicit report limitation; their raw originals are not rewritten to create historical certainty.
